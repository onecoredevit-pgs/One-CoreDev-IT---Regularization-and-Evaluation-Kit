Performance Management Guide

What you need to do — and when — to manage your team properly.

As your Employer of Record, we handle the legal employment. You manage the work. This kit gives you every document, template, and deadline to stay compliant with Philippine labor law — from Day 1 through regularization.

⚠

Critical legal reminder: Under Article 296 of the Philippine Labor Code, reasonable performance standards must be communicated to the employee at the time of engagement. Failure to do so results in automatic regularization, regardless of actual performance.
